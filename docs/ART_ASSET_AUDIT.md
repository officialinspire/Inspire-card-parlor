- Character, item, UI, stamp, map-icon and VFX sheets have real transparency. Several keep stray colour under fully transparent pixels; the pipeline zeroes it so sprite edges don't pick up coloured fringes when scaled.
- Ghost characters (Mary Ball Washington, George Washington) and VFX use translucent glows. Cells are cut on opaque artwork only, so the glow is shared at a cell boundary rather than a figure being cut; faint glow below alpha 8 is dropped.
- The documents (receipts, commission form, dossier, sketchbook page, letter, puzzle diagram, the two Thirteenth Hand cards) sit on soft, partly transparent paper edges; check them over both dark and light backgrounds before use.
- The two card backs are opaque images; the build cuts each card out of its white (navy-gold) or black (tricorn) surround, keeping the rounded corners.
- All nine new gameboards are opaque RGB images, as boards should be.

## Unverified artwork

- **All sprite names are mine, from looking at the art.** Character pose names are slot conventions (row = portrait / cards / full body); the meaning of the `special`, `signature` and `gesture-*` slots differs per character (e.g. Cheryl's magnifier, Sophia's binoculars, George Washington's spectral card) and is not confirmed by the artist.
- Item names (e.g. `triangle-sigil-coin`, `cipher-ribbon`, `key-13-tag`) describe what is drawn, not how the story uses it. The story meaning of every item, stamp, effect and map icon is unverified.
- The twelve passport stamps are named after what they show; which chapter or character each belongs to is my reading, not the artist's.
- Map location tiles are identified by their file names; their placement on the river map is not defined anywhere yet.
- **Freemasonry** (Engraved Georgian faces + navy-gold back) and **George Washington's Ghost** (Spectral Washington faces + tricorn back) were checked card by card in Prompt 19: rank and suit of all 52 cards by their top-left index, pips and colours. Misprinted bottom-right indices (Freemasonry: the four 9s and K♠; Washington: 9♠, 9♥, 9♦, 9♣, 5♥, 6♣, K♥) are replaced at build time by each card's own top-left index rotated 180° (`indexFixes` in their atlas.json). The four Freemasonry 5s show four pips (not changed; disclosed). The pairing of these faces with these backs is my reading of the art.
- **INSPIRE Botanical Cannabis deck** (audited only): the 8 of diamonds reads "7" in its top-left index and shows a heart in its bottom-right corner; the 9 of diamonds also shows a heart in its bottom-right corner; the 3 of spades reads "2" in its top-left index (three pips, bottom index 3). The deck is cannabis-themed and should be treated as adult content (isolated like the other 18+ decks) if it is added.
- Nothing in `public/assets/story/` is used by the game yet, and nothing is marked verified (`verified: false` on every sheet).
- **Dante's expression row** is drawn in a different order from the other sheets: surprise (with alarm marks), then thinking (hand on chin), then a sideways smirk. Its names in `sources.json` now follow the drawings (checked by eye in the upgrade series); the other six sheets match the standard order.
